## Description

> ## What this does
>
> **1. Receipts and Ask no longer fail on a phone when Gemini or Genie is slow or busy.**
> Both worked from the command line but failed through the phone, because three timeouts stacked up in front of the API: the app gave a receipt scan 30 s, the web proxy gave any API call 30 s (Next's default), and the API waited 60 s on Gemini. One slow or busy Gemini attempt, or a Genie answer over 30 s (a waking warehouse), was cut off before it could finish. The log showed three receipt requests cancelled at about 30 s each, and a Gemini read timeout.
> - The web proxy now waits up to 180 s (`experimental.proxyTimeout`). Reproduced with a 40 s fake API: HTTP 500 at 31 s before, 200 at 40 s after.
> - A receipt scan gets 120 s in the app; the other intake calls keep their 30 s.
> - The Gemini extractor gives the main model 40 s, and on a rate limit (429), a server error or a stall it tries the lighter `gemini-3.1-flash-lite` (25 s), up to three attempts in all. `GEMINI_FALLBACK_MODEL` sets or disables it. A bad request or key (400, 403) is still reported at once.
> - New tests cover the fail-over (rate limit, stall, giving up, fallback off) and the longer intake timeout.
>
> **2. Freebuff is removed from the README**: its section and card, its row in the prizes table, and the mentions in the intro, the banner strip and the Devpost chips. The art is rebuilt without it.
>
> ## Checked
> - `ruff` clean; `pytest` 301 passed; `tsc` and `eslint` clean; `vitest` 54 passed; the full Playwright suite (26 tests) passed against an isolated copy of the app.
> - Live through the web proxy with a phone-sized photo: the receipt scan took 15 s and Ask 14 s.
>
> ## Notes
> - Gemini 3.5 Flash is intermittent (we saw a 429, a stall, and an earlier hang on another model), so the fail-over matters more than the timeouts alone.
> - A sleeping laptop also freezes the API, web app and tunnel together; that is separate from this fix.
>
